# Methodology

`oss-scorecard` is built from three layers of assets:

1. **Benchmarks** — 6 domains × 37 leaves describing what strong OSS projects do well.
2. **Case studies** — real repositories, including a 2–6 month explosive-growth cohort.
3. **Playbooks** — actionable fixes that convert weak scores into concrete next steps.

## Six domains

- `facade`
- `docs`
- `community`
- `quality`
- `caselib`
- `tooling`

## Evaluation philosophy

- configuration-driven
- hard-fail on missing critical structure
- public-evidence-first
- machine fields remain canonical and stable
- recommendations should map score gaps to actionable playbooks
